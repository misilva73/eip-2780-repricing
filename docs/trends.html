How each client's numbers move between benchmark runs. Lower is cheaper — a falling line or a green delta means a client's proposed gas (or its underlying runtime) came down since the previous run. Lines are split per receiver case: colour identifies the client, line style the case.

Mixed gas anchors across this history. Proposed gas is a measured runtime scaled by a fixed throughput anchor, and that anchor changed between runs: 100 Mgas/s from 2026-05-29 23:44 to 2026-07-20 16:52; 75 Mgas/s from 2026-07-31 05:36 to 2026-08-02 23:21. Older runs are left as analyzed rather than rescaled, so part of the step in the Proposed gas lines — and in the since-last-run delta below — at each boundary is the anchor change, not a client getting faster or slower. Switch the metric to Runtime (ms) for an anchor-independent view.
